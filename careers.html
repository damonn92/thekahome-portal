    Operational Specialist (Mandarin and English)

    • Irvine, CA
    • Full-time
    • On-site
    • Entry level
    Apply on LinkedIn(opens in a new tab)

    About the role

    We’re looking for a detail-oriented, reliable person to join our operations team. The work is mainly computer-based: account setup, subscription management, data entry and daily operational tasks.

    Responsibilities

    • Set up and configure cloud and SaaS accounts following established procedures
    • Process account provisioning and subscription-related requests
    • Keep account and order records accurate
    • Monitor account status and flag potential issues
    • Follow internal SOPs and operational checklists
    • Maintain records in Excel, Google Sheets and internal tools
    • Carry out routine quality-control checks
    • Update the operations team on account and order status
    • Handle confidential business information responsibly
    • Comply with platform terms of service, verification requirements and payment policies

    Qualifications

    • Bilingual in Mandarin Chinese and English, written and spoken (required)
    • Strong attention to detail and the ability to follow procedures
    • Comfortable with computers and web-based applications
    • Basic Excel or Google Sheets
    • Reliable and accurate on repetitive operational tasks
    • Prior experience in SaaS operations, e-commerce, customer support or account operations is a plus
    • No programming experience needed

    Benefits

    • 401(k) with matching
    • Health, dental, vision and life insurance
    • Paid time off
