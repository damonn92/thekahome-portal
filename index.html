01Services

Three service lines. One accountable team.

Each line stands on its own. Together they cover the whole path, from the first account to AI running in production.

S/01Operate

AI & Cloud Account Operations

We provide and run the AI, cloud and SaaS accounts your team depends on — so seats, plans and renewals stop being somebody’s side job.

Format
Ongoing, day-to-day operations
Best for
Teams that rely on many AI, cloud and SaaS tools and seats

Scope

  • Setup and configuration of AI, cloud and SaaS accounts
  • Provisioning and subscription requests
  • Seat, plan and renewal management
  • Billing and order record-keeping
  • Account-status monitoring and early issue flagging

What you get

  • An SOP-driven operations team handling your requests
  • Routine quality-control checks on the work
  • Clean records of accounts, billing and orders
  • An early flag when an account needs attention

Accounts under this service are held and managed by Kahome Inc. We give your team the access it needs and bill you for the seats and subscriptions you use. The underlying platforms set their own terms and availability.
