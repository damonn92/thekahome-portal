Services

Three service lines. One accountable team.

Each line stands on its own. Together they cover the whole path, from the first account to AI running in production.

01Operate

AI & Cloud Account Operations

We provide and run the AI, cloud and SaaS accounts your team depends on — so seats, plans and renewals stop being somebody’s side job.

  • Setup and configuration of AI, cloud and SaaS accounts
  • Provisioning and subscription requests
  • Seat, plan and renewal management
  • Billing and order record-keeping
  • Account-status monitoring and early issue flagging

Accounts under this service are held and managed by Kahome Inc. We give your team the access it needs and bill you for the seats and subscriptions you use. The underlying platforms set their own terms and availability.

Discuss account operations

02Access

AI Model & API Access

We get developers and teams up and running with leading AI models and APIs — then keep keys, usage and spend visible, with the aim that month-end holds no surprises.

  • Access setup for leading AI models and APIs
  • Key and usage management
  • Usage monitoring and cost visibility
  • Consolidated billing
  • Bilingual technical support

Access is provided through accounts and keys that Kahome Inc holds and manages. We issue access to your team and bill you for usage. The underlying platforms set their own terms and availability. Ask us which models and platforms we currently support.

Discuss model and API access

03Build

Enterprise AI Implementation

AI agents, workflow automation, custom development and integrations — scoped around the work your business actually does, and taken from discovery and pilot through deployment and ongoing support.

  • AI agents
  • Workflow automation
  • Custom development
  • Integrations with the tools and systems you already use

We start small on purpose. A pilot shows whether an agent or automation pays off before you commit to a full rollout.
